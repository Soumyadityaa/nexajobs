Data Protection & Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At NexaJobs, we are deeply committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of our users. This Privacy Policy delineates our practices regarding the collection, utilization, disclosure, and protection of your information when you interact with our digital platform. By utilizing the NexaJobs portal, you consent to the data practices described in this document.

1. Information Collection

We collect diverse categories of information to optimize our services, including:

  • Personal Data: Information such as your name, email address, professional credentials, and resume submissions voluntarily provided via contact forms or subscription models.
  • Local Storage Data: Data pertaining to your saved job listings and application states within the Kanban tracker, which is stored locally within your browser environment.
  • Diagnostic Data: Non-identifiable metrics including browser type, operating system, and interaction timestamps collected automatically to ensure system stability.

2. Utilization of Data

We deploy the collected data exclusively to enhance user experience and operational efficiency. Specific uses include:

  • Facilitating customer support and responding to corporate inquiries.
  • Distributing automated, relevant job notifications for users opted into our subscription service.
  • Powering the frontend AI Resume Matcher logic securely within your active browser session.

3. Data Security & Storage

NexaJobs employs enterprise-grade administrative, technical, and physical security protocols to mitigate the risk of unauthorized access or data breaches. Notably, features such as the Application Tracker and AI Resume Matcher are engineered to run client-side, meaning your sensitive professional data is processed locally and is not transmitted to external servers without explicit consent.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

Our portal frequently links to external corporate application tracking systems (ATS) and government employment websites. NexaJobs does not govern the privacy protocols of these third-party environments. Candidates are strongly advised to scrutinize the respective privacy frameworks of external employers before submitting personal documentation.

5. Policy Amendments

We reserve the right to amend this Privacy Policy to reflect technological advancements or regulatory shifts. Updates will be published on this URL, and the "Effective Date" will be revised accordingly.

Trust & Safety
